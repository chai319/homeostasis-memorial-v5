## 表情別の生成指示

- `happy` — Warm natural smile, eyes bright and gently relaxed, small parted smiling mouth. Readable happiness, restrained for this character.
- `blush` — Romantically flustered, clearly rosy cheeks, slightly shy averted eyes, tiny hesitant smile, brows softened. No tears.
- `worried` — Worried about a friend, inner eyebrows lifted and knitted, eyes uncertain, mouth slightly tense and parted.
- `surprised` — Clearly surprised, eyebrows lifted, visible eye widened, small open mouth. Keep all hair in its original place.
- `angry` — Quiet irritation, eyebrows drawn down, sharp focused eyes, lips pressed, controlled anger.
- `rage` — Strong genuine anger, brows sharply knitted, visibly intense eyes and open mouth speaking firmly. No comic symbols or distortion.
- `listless` — Sleepy, tired and unenergetic, half-lidded eyes, slack relaxed mouth, level brows.
- `depressed` — Dejected after a setback, gaze lowered, inward raised brows, corners of the mouth down, no tears.
- `playful` — Playful teasing, slightly raised eyebrow, sideways lively glance, small mischievous asymmetric smile.
- `trust` — Open heartfelt trust, gentle steady gaze toward viewer, brow fully relaxed, small vulnerable affectionate smile.
- `joyful_tears` — Overcome with happy relief, smiling warmly while small tears visibly pool in eyes and one thin tear runs down cheek.
- `tears` — Quietly crying, wet eyes with visible clear tear tracks, trembling mouth, raised inner brows. Still dignified.
- `sorrow` — Deep sorrow, downcast sorrowful eyes, inward arched brows, closed tight mouth with sadness. More composed than crying.
